# Agent 5 — Lineage & Descendants (raw findings)

## Name & heraldry (shareable jewel)
- "Talbot" linked to the **talbot hound** (extinct white hunting dog, ancestor of bloodhound/beagle). Henry VI reportedly called the 1st Earl "Talbott, oure good dogge."
- **Arms:** *Gules, a lion rampant within a bordure engrailed Or* — actually the arms of Welsh princess **Gwenllian ferch Rhys Mechyll** (d/o Rhys Mechyll d.1244), wife of Gilbert Talbot (d.1274); adopted by the Talbots.
- **Supporters:** two white Talbot hounds. **Motto:** *Prest d'accomplir* ("Ready to accomplish").
- **Seats:** **Ingestre Hall** (Chetwynd-Talbot branch, Staffs), **Alton Towers** (Shrewsbury Earls, Staffs; sold 1924), **Hensol Castle** (Baron Talbot, Glamorgan).

## Ancestor line (Gerald → 1st Earl of Shrewsbury) — ~15 generations
- Norman origins: Richard Talbot (Domesday 1086) → Barons Talbot → **John Talbot, 1st Earl of Shrewsbury (c.1387–1453)**, "the English Achilles," KG, also 1st Earl of Waterford; Shakespeare's *Henry VI Pt 1*; killed at **Battle of Castillon, 17 Jul 1453** (last battle of the Hundred Years' War); son Lisle died beside him.
- 1st Earl → 2nd Earl (d.1460, killed at Northampton) → **Sir Gilbert Talbot KG (1452–1518)** [3rd son; fought for Henry Tudor at Bosworth 1485] — the **cadet-branch root**.
- Cadet line: Sir Gilbert → Sir John of Albrighton → Grafton Talbots → Sherrington Talbot (1577–1642) → William of Stourton Castle → **Bishop William Talbot** (1659–1730; Bp Oxford/Salisbury/Durham) → **Charles Talbot, 1st Baron Talbot of Hensol (1685–1737), Lord Chancellor** → William, 1st Earl Talbot (1st creation) → Hon. John Talbot (judge; m. Catherine Chetwynd) → **John Chetwynd-Talbot, 1st Earl Talbot (2nd creation, 1749–1793)** [took name Chetwynd 1786 on inheriting Ingestre] → **Charles Chetwynd-Talbot, 2nd Earl Talbot (1777–1849)** → **Rev. Hon. William Whitworth Chetwynd-Talbot (1814–1888)** [6th son; Gerald's grandfather] → **Lt-Col Gerald Francis Talbot (1848–1904)** → **Sir Gerald Francis Talbot (1881–1945)**.
- **Confidence:** All links 17th c. onward DOCUMENTED. Medieval (Sir Gilbert→1st Earl) DOCUMENTED. **One weak link:** John of Salwarpe (c.1549) as son of Sir John of Grafton (1545–1611) — asserted by Stirnet/WikiTree, not line-by-line proven (needs Heralds' Visitations of Worcs).

## Shrewsbury connection (real & vivid)
- Gerald's great-great-uncle **Henry John Chetwynd-Talbot** (brother of Rev. William Whitworth) became **18th Earl of Shrewsbury in 1856**, reuniting Talbot & Shrewsbury earldoms. Gerald = 1st cousin once removed of the 18th Earl. Present **22nd Earl, Charles Chetwynd-Talbot (b.1952)** = Gerald's 4th cousin; Premier Earl of England & Ireland.
- Notable kin: **Eleanor Talbot** (alleged secret wife of Edward IV → Princes in the Tower illegitimacy); **George, 6th Earl** (custodian of Mary Queen of Scots; m. Bess of Hardwick); **Charles, 12th Earl/1st Duke of Shrewsbury** (Immortal Seven, 1688).

## Maternal Bradhurst (American) line
- Henrietta Clarissa Noyes Bradhurst (d.1928) = dau. of **Henry Maunsell Bradhurst (1822–1894)** (moved family to England 1865; leased Faulkbourne Hall, Essex) & Elizabeth Tracy (Noyes).
- Back via John Maunsell Bradhurst → **Dr Samuel Bradhurst III (1749–1826)**: Revolutionary War surgeon (Princeton, Brandywine); **friend & neighbour of Alexander Hamilton** (sold him 20 acres for Hamilton Grange 1799). Estate "Pinehurst," upper Manhattan → **Bradhurst Avenue, Harlem** named for the family.
- CAVEAT: "friends of Hamilton" = DOCUMENTED; "descended from / blood relation of Hamilton" = **UNVERIFIED, almost certainly false** — do NOT state as fact.
- Augustus Maunsell Bradhurst's 1910 book *My Forefathers* (archive.org) = best Bradhurst source (not fully read).
