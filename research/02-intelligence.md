## Intelligence context of Athens post
- **Compton Mackenzie** ran British intelligence (MI1c, forerunner of SIS) in Athens 1915–17; built 24,000-name surveillance archive; founded Aegean Intelligence Service 1917. Expelled after the *Noemvriana* (Dec 1916). Talbot arrived in the **post-Mackenzie phase**.
- Naval Attachés reported to the **Naval Intelligence Division (NID)** under DNI "Blinker" Hall — structural, not personal, intelligence link.
- 1919–22: Greco-Turkish War; Britain officially neutral, Lloyd George privately pro-Greek (per intercepted Greek traffic, TNA HW 12). Athens attaché = key observer.
- Friendship with **Venizelos** documented (basis of the 1922 mission).
