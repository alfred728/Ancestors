# Agent 3 — The Prince Andrew Rescue (1922) (raw findings)

## Context: why Andrew was in danger
- **National Schism (1914–):** Venizelists (pro-Allied) vs Royalists (King Constantine I, the Kaiser's brother-in-law — widely seen as pro-German). 1917 Allied proclamation: "Berlin has commanded Athens." Royal family expelled 1917; Talbot arrived as Naval Attaché **23 Aug 1917**, part of the Allied apparatus.
- **Asia Minor Catastrophe (1922):** Greek defeat; Smyrna burned 9 Sept 1922. Andrew had commanded **II Army Corps at the Battle of the Sakarya (Aug 1921)**; refused an order from Gen. Papoulas → later scapegoated.
- **Sept 1922 Revolution:** Plastiras & Gonatas seize power; Constantine abdicates.
- **Trial of the Six** (13–27 Nov): six royalist leaders → executed by firing squad at Goudi, **28 Nov 1922** ("judicial murder," per Curzon). Same day Lindley warns Curzon that Andrew is in mortal danger.
- Pangalos (War Minister) to Andrew in his cell: *"Poor things, what a pity they will soon be orphans!"*

## King George V's role
- Haunted by abandoning Tsar Nicholas II (murdered 1918); per Kenneth Rose, *"He didn't want any more blood on his hands."* Pressed Bonar Law & Curzon to act. Appeal reached him via Princess Alice → her brother Louis (Mountbatten).

## Talbot's mission
- Chosen because: former Naval Attaché (since 1917), knew Plastiras/Gonatas/Pangalos from Venizelist days, **personal friend of Venizelos**, and could act covertly (unofficial, former attaché).
- Arrived Athens **30 Nov 1922** (2 days after the executions).
- Negotiated with Gonatas (PM), Pangalos (War Min), Plastiras (strongman), Rentis (acting FM): no further executions + Andrew to be tried, sentenced to exile, pardoned, released to Talbot. **Utmost secrecy** — even the warship captain not told the purpose.
- Visited Andrew in prison (no verbatim record).

## Court martial & evacuation
- Court martial **1–3 Dec 1922**, Chamber of Deputies. Charge: disobeying battlefield orders at Sakarya; Papoulas testified for prosecution. Andrew (in civilian clothes) pleaded he waited for III Corps coordination that never came.
- **3 Dec 1922:** guilty; stripped of rank, **perpetual banishment**; Plastiras grants pardon; Andrew released to Talbot.
- **HMS Calypso** (Caledon-class light cruiser; Capt. **Herbert A. Buchanan-Wollaston**) from **Phaleron Bay**; Alice already aboard → sailed to **Corfu** for the 5 children (daughters Margarita 17, Theodora 16, Cecilie 12, Sophie 6; **Prince Philip ~18 months, carried aboard in a cot made from an orange box**).
- **5 Dec 1922:** reached **Brindisi**; British Ambassador advanced funds → Rome (Andrew thanked the Pope & Cardinal Gasparri, who'd urged clemency) → Paris → Saint-Cloud.

## Recognition
- **14 Dec 1922:** George V personally appointed Talbot **KCVO** "for services rendered to Prince Andrew of Greece" — the RVO is the Sovereign's personal gift (no Cabinet involvement); awarded within ~2 weeks.
- Bentinck (Athens chargé): Talbot's brief stay "had played an important part in staving off more executions."

## Aftermath of the family
- Andrew & Alice's marriage dissolved; Alice institutionalised 1930–32. Four daughters married German aristocrats 1930–31 (Sophie's husband Christoph of Hesse = NSDAP/Waffen-SS; Cecilie d. 1937 air crash). Philip raised in Britain by Mountbattens; sisters not invited to his 1947 wedding (Nazi associations).
- Andrew died **3 Dec 1944** in Monte Carlo (22 years to the day after the verdict).

## "GERMANS" PUZZLE — VERDICT
- **No German involvement in 1922** whatsoever (Weimar Germany had no role; the Revolutionary Committee was the anti-royalist, *anti*-German Venizelist faction). [DOCUMENTED negative]
- **Most likely origin of the family lore (INFERENCE, strongly supported):** Andrew's own faction — the Greek **royalists** — were *the* "pro-German party" (Constantine = Kaiser's brother-in-law; Andrew accused in the Commons of being a German agent; family expelled 1917 for German sympathies). Oral history compressed "rescued a pro-German prince from the Greek revolutionaries" → "rescued from the Germans."
- **Reinforcing layers:** Princess Alice's German/Battenberg birth (her father Prince Louis forced to anglicise to Mountbatten in 1914); Andrew's 4 daughters all married Germans (3 with Nazi ties); Philip schooled in Germany (Salem); the story most vividly retold during WWII. 
- **Framing for the report:** lore is literally inexact but "captures something real" — the people Talbot rescued were genuinely the side branded "German" for a decade. Treat with respect.
